# Career Vision Statement Activity

This activity guides you through a structured writing process to develop a meaningful Career Vision Statement. You will build it in layers — starting with raw material from your previous work, developing a draft, refining it, and then committing to a final version.

---

## Step 1 — Gather Your Raw Material

Before writing, pull together the following from your previous work:

- Your Personal Empathy Map insights
- Your SWOT Analysis
- Your Personal POV Statement
- Your RIASEC Profile
- Your Career Exploration Map

---

## Step 2 — Answer the Foundation Questions

**What kind of work do I want to do?**
*(Think about tasks, not job titles. What activities do you want to fill your professional days with? What problems do you want to work on?)*
[I want to do meaningful work, stuff that can be remembered and respected]

**Who do I want to serve or impact?**
*(Who are the people, communities, or sectors your work will benefit? Be as specific as you can.)*
[I want to impact everyone]

**What values do I want my career to embody?**
[I want respect and joy from my work]

**What kind of professional do I want to become?**
*(What qualities, character traits, or reputation do you want to be known for in your field?)*
[I want to be known for being a hardworker and to be humble and respected for it]

**What does success look like for me — not financially, but meaningfully?**
*(Set salary aside for a moment. What would make you feel that your career was truly worthwhile?)*
[If I can look back and know I've done good for people]

---

## Step 3 — Draft Your Career Vision Statement

Using your foundation questions as raw material, write a first draft of your Career Vision Statement. Aim for 3–5 sentences. Use the structure below as a guide — but feel free to depart from it once you have a draft:

> "My career vision is to [type of work] for/with [who you serve], so that [impact or outcome you want to create]. I want to be known as a professional who [qualities/values]. I see myself building a career in [field or sector] where I can [specific contribution], grounded in my commitment to [core value]."

**My Draft Career Vision Statement:**
[My career vision is to [do meaningful work with technology] for/with [my community], so that [Everyone can know and repect my line of work]. I want to be known as a professional who [can be proud of the job]. I see myself building a career in [cyber/ai] where I can [protect and be smart], grounded in my commitment to [my self].]

---

## Step 4 — Refine and Finalize

Read your draft aloud (or read it slowly in your head). Ask yourself:

- Does this sound like me, or does it sound like what I think I should say?
- Is it specific enough to be meaningful?
- Is it broad enough to allow for growth?
- Does it excite me when I read it?

Revise as needed and write your final version below.

**My Final Career Vision Statement:**

> [My career vision is to [do meaningful work with technology] for/with [my community], so that [Everyone can know and repect my line of work]. I want to be known as a professional who [can be proud of the job]. I see myself building a career in [cyber/ai] where I can [protect and be smart], grounded in my commitment to [my self].]

---

💡 **Note:** Your Career Vision Statement is a living document. You are not locked into it forever. You will revisit and revise it in Module 5 and beyond as your self-knowledge deepens and your career exploration continues.
